## How Raid AI Works

Deepfakes are one of the fastest-growing cybersecurity threats. AI can now clone voices from seconds of audio, generate convincing face swaps in real-time, and produce entirely synthetic images and video. Raid AI detects these across all three modalities through a four-step process:

1. Upload or Stream Media. Submit audio files, images, or video through the dashboard, or stream live from meetings (Teams, Zoom, Meet, Slack, Webex). Send voice notes via WhatsApp.
2. AI-Powered Analysis. Proprietary ML models analyze frequency patterns, vocal biomarkers, visual artifacts, facial inconsistencies, and temporal anomalies to determine authenticity.
3. Real-Time Verdict. Instant authenticity verdict with confidence score. Deepfake segments are flagged during live calls and meetings.
4. Report and Act. Forensic reports with timestamps, compliance exports, and automated SOC alerts. All media is discarded after processing.

## Key Definitions

- Deepfake: Synthetic media (audio, image, or video) generated by AI to impersonate a real person or fabricate content.
- Deepfake audio: AI-generated speech designed to sound like a specific human speaker.
- Deepfake image: AI-generated or manipulated images including face swaps, fully synthetic faces, and tampered photographs.
- Deepfake video: Face-swapped, lip-synced, or fully AI-generated video content designed to impersonate real people.
- Voice cloning: AI replication of a target person's voice from as little as three seconds of sample audio.
- Face swap: Replacing one person's face with another in an image or video using AI.
- Voice phishing (vishing): Phone-based social engineering attacks that now frequently incorporate deepfake audio.
- CEO fraud: Fraud scheme that impersonates a company executive using cloned voices or deepfake video.
